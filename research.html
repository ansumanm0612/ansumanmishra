Research

Thesis

  • Mishra, A. Causal Architecture, Actuality & Negation in Hindi-Urdu Modality. MA thesis, The English and Foreign Languages University, Hyderabad.

    Analyses the sak-/pā- modal distinction through causal premise semantics, event-relativity, and two-dimensional projection. The thesis argues for a distinction between the causal structure associated with pā- and the permission-related interpretation of sak-.
