About me

I was born in Odisha but have called Hyderabad home for the last 20 years. I started out as a science student, convinced that physics and chemistry were my future. Somewhere along the way, though, I realized that I was more interested in analyzing sentences and meaning than chemical bonds. That eventually led me to the University of Hyderabad (UoH), where I completed my Bachelor's in Linguistics.

I recently completed my Master's in Computational Linguistics at the English and Foreign Languages University (EFLU), Hyderabad. I started out working primarily in syntax, but my interests have since moved toward semantics and pragmatics, particularly modality, particles, and not-at-issue content. More broadly, I am interested in how meaning depends on context and how much of what we understand goes beyond what is said because humans rarely say exactly what they mean :-)

When I am not doing linguistics, I am usually watching a cricket or chess match, writing poetry, reading philosophy, learning facts with almost no practical value, or doom-scrolling through Twitter/X for the banter.
