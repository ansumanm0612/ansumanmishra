Linguist & Researcher

I am a linguist working at the intersection of formal linguistics and computational approaches to language, with a primary focus on the semantics–pragmatics interface. I recently completed my Master's in Computational Linguistics at The English and Foreign Languages University (EFLU), Hyderabad.

My research focuses on formal semantics and pragmatics in South Asian languages, particularly Odia, Telugu, and Hindi. I am interested in how linguistic expressions interact with context and pragmatic inference, with particular interests in modality, evidentiality, discourse particles, and not-at-issue content.

Research interests

Formal semantics, pragmatics, the semantics–pragmatics interface, modality, evidentiality, discourse particles, and linguistic typology, with a focus on South Asian languages.
